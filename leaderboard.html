Key Takeaways
5th

Denver's National Ranking

Ahead of San Diego, LA, Miami, Houston, Chicago, and Seattle. Only the desert Southwest beats it.

+21%

vs. Miami

Denver gets 21% more direct beam sunlight than Miami. Humidity and clouds scatter Miami's solar radiation significantly.

+7%

vs. Los Angeles

Denver beats LA in DNI by 7%. Mile-high altitude and dry air more than compensate for higher latitude.

+75%

vs. Seattle

Denver gets 75% more direct sunlight than Seattle. Completely different league for solar production.

5,280 ft

The Altitude Effect

Above ~15% of Earth's atmosphere. Less air = less scattering = more direct beam energy hitting panels.

<90°F

Temperature Advantage

Panels lose ~0.4%/°C above 25°C. Phoenix hits 115°F; Denver rarely exceeds 90°F. Cooler panels produce more per unit of sunlight.
